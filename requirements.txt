## What it does

* Solves the vector loop equations for a planar four-bar linkage numerically using `scipy.optimize.fsolve`
* Calculates the position of the coupler and rocker for a given crank angle
* Sweeps through a full 360° crank rotation to analyse the complete mechanism motion
* Checks **Grashof's condition** to determine whether the mechanism is capable of continuous crank rotation
* Performs **geometric feasibility checking** to identify link-length combinations that cannot form a valid closed linkage
* Calculates the **transmission angle** throughout the crank cycle and highlights regions outside the commonly used 40°,140° range
* Provides an interactive explorer with adjustable sliders for **all four link lengths**
* Provides a **crank-angle slider** for manually controlling the mechanism's position
* Includes **automatic rotation** with play/pause controls for observing the mechanism through its full cycle
* Displays live feedback on the mechanism's **Grashof condition and geometric validity** as the link dimensions are changed
* Plots the **transmission angle** against crank angle to help evaluate mechanical performance throughout the cycle
* Plots the **vertical motion of the coupler-rocker joint** through the crank cycle
* Updates the mechanism geometry and analysis plots **in real time** as the inputs are changed
* Includes a separate animation script for generating a **GIF demonstration** of the linkage motion and transmission-angle behaviour
